Firewood

Seasoned slowly, burns cleanly

Every log is stacked in purpose built sheds for up to a year, until its moisture content is 20% or less.

Dry wood burns more efficiently, which means more heat for your money and less smoke in the air. Air dried wood also carries a low carbon footprint. Logs are cut to a standard size, or to order to fit your stove or boiler.

We started in 1990 supplying firewood to tenants on the estate. Today we are one of the leading suppliers of sustainable firewood in the South Lakes, and registered on the Biomass Suppliers List for virgin, naturally seasoned firewood.

Conservation

Woods managed for wildlife first

A large part of our woodland is designated as Sites of Special Scientific Interest. We manage it with the Forestry Commission and Butterfly Conservation to protect some of the country's most endangered species.

Coppicing

An ancient practice of thinning plots in rotation. Light reaches the woodland floor, violets and primroses return, and butterflies follow.

Fritillaries

The High Brown Fritillary has vanished from 94% of its UK sites in fifty years. Our woods are among the places it still survives.

Red squirrels

Since 2016 we have worked with the Westmorland Red Squirrel Society to bring red squirrels back to Witherslack.

Biomass

Heating the estate from its own woods

In 2011 we installed the estate's first biomass boiler in the woodyard at Halecat.

The 200kW boiler heats Halecat's homes, offices and our woodland operations, using around 200 tonnes of wood a year. It runs on faggots, the bundles of branches and twigs left over from forestry work.

The trees we harvest are coppiced or replanted, so the fuel is constantly renewed and the growing trees take up almost as much carbon as is released when the wood is burned. Visitors are welcome at the yard.
