The house

In the family since the early 1800s

Halecat has been in the Stanley family since it was built. The furnishings are original, and we spent our childhoods here. There is nowhere else quite like it.

Four generous bedrooms sleep eleven, with room for little ones if you ask. The original bathrooms are part of the charm, and you will not beat our loo for a view.

On a wet day there are squishy sofas, a library, a piano for family songs, board games and a real fire. On a fine one, the large enclosed garden and the woods beyond are yours.

Weddings, events & parties

Garden, trail & studios

A creative corner of the estate

The East Wing of the house is home to artists' studios. Painters, a potter and sculptors work here, and open their doors for small events and open studio days.

When it runs, the Halecat Garden Sculpture Trail winds for a mile through the woods around the house, with work by estate artists and sculptors from across Cumbria. Most pieces are for sale and made for the outdoors.

Camping & extra guests

Room for everyone

Extra guests are welcome to camp in the garden or in the orchard, a popular choice for parties and weddings.

Nearby, Moss Howe Farm offers bell tents, yurts and pods with heated showers and a farm shop, and Witherslack Hall Equestrian Centre has farmhouse B&B and a bunk barn. Both are around two miles from the house.
